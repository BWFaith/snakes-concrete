Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. Estimates are free for every project. Use the booking page for a price range in about two minutes, then we confirm the final number with a quick site visit.
How long before I can walk or drive on new concrete?
Light foot traffic is usually fine after 24 to 48 hours. Keep vehicles off for about 7 days, and heavy loads for 28 days when concrete reaches full design strength.
How thick should a driveway be?
Four inches is standard for cars. Five or six inches is better for trucks, trailers and heavy equipment. We recommend a thickness for your use when we quote.
Will my concrete crack?
All concrete can crack as it cures and shrinks. We place control joints so any cracking happens along the saw-cut lines, and we prepare the base to reduce settling.
Do you pour in hot or cold weather?
Yes, with the right steps. Cold pours need protection from freezing, and hot pours need extra attention to mix and curing. We will move a date if conditions are not safe for the concrete.
Do I need a permit?
Some projects do, especially foundations, retaining walls and work near the road. We will tell you early what your project needs.
What areas do you serve?
Georgia, Tennessee and Alabama. See the service areas page for the towns we cover most.
Can you match or repair existing concrete?
We can often replace sections or add to existing concrete. Color matching is limited, so we explain what to expect during the estimate.
Do you reinforce concrete with wire mesh or rebar?
Reinforcement is chosen to suit the project. You can add wire mesh or rebar in the quote calculator, and we recommend what fits your slab.
How do I book?
Use the booking page to build your quote and choose a date, or call or message us on WhatsApp.
