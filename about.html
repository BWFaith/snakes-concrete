A family-run crew that takes pride in the pour

Snakes Concrete LLC builds driveways, slabs, patios and foundations across Georgia, Tennessee and Alabama.

Concrete done the way we would want it done at home

Snakes Concrete LLC is owned and run by Fernando and Nallely Martinez out of Rossville, Georgia. We work for homeowners, builders and businesses, and we put the same care into a back-door walkway as we do into a commercial pour.

Our approach is simple: prepare the ground properly, pour with a crew that knows the work, and finish the surface for how it will be used. We quote clearly, show up when we say, and leave the site clean.

Our promise is on the flyer and on every job: pouring quality you can trust.
