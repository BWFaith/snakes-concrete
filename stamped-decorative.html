Stamped and decorative concrete

Concrete does not have to be boring. Choose a pattern, a color and a sealer, and get a surface that looks custom and wears like concrete.

The look of stone and wood, poured in place

Stamped concrete is colored, textured and pressed with patterns while it is still workable, then sealed. The result is a patio, porch or drive that looks custom and stays low maintenance.

  • Patios and porchesCreate an outdoor room your family actually uses.
  • Entries and walkwaysMake the first impression on your forever home.
  • Driveways and pool areasAdd pattern and texture where it shows most.

How a stamped pour works

  1. Prep and form

    Compact the base, set forms and place reinforcement for a stable slab.

  2. Pour and color

    Place and level the concrete, then add color so the tone is even.

  3. Stamp and texture

    Press the pattern at exactly the right moment, then add texture and detail.

  4. Cure and seal

    Clean, cut joints and seal to protect the color and surface.

Care for a stamped surface

  • Reseal about every 2 to 3 yearsSealer protects color and slows wear. Schedule depends on traffic and sun.
  • Clean with mild soap and waterAvoid harsh chemicals and metal-blade shovels.
  • Keep de-icing salts offUse sand for traction instead.
How long does stamped concrete last?
With good prep, sealing and basic care, stamped concrete lasts for years like regular concrete, with the pattern and color staying vibrant.
Is stamped concrete slippery?
Sealers can be slick when wet, so we can add a non-slip additive for pool areas, steps and slopes.
Can I stamp over existing concrete?
Sometimes, with an overlay system. We check the condition of your slab during the estimate.
